Everything you've outgrown is something someone nearby is looking for. Everything they've outgrown might be exactly what you want. Swapt exists to close that loop — so your closet stops being clutter and starts being currency.

How we make it true

Four rules every swap lives by

The belief only means something if the app behaves like it. So these principles sit behind every decision we make.

Every match is a two-sided yes

Nothing happens unless both people want in. No lowballing, no haggling strangers, no pressure. A swap is mutual excitement or it isn't a swap.

Money-free by design

There's no price field anywhere in Swapt — deliberately. The moment money enters, it's a marketplace. Value is decided by two people agreeing, not by a number.

Local first

Your neighborhood's stuff circulating within your neighborhood. Handovers over shipping labels. Neighbors over couriers.

A game, not a chore

Swiping, matching, the reveal — trading should feel like play. If a flow starts to feel like admin, it's off-brand and we redesign it.
