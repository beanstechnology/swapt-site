2Scope of this policy

This policy applies to the Swapt mobile app, any related websites we operate, and the accounts, listings, matches, and messages created through them (together, the “Service”). It does not cover third-party services you might reach from Swapt — for example, if you choose to add a link to your Instagram, Facebook, or X profile, those platforms have their own privacy policies that govern what happens once you leave Swapt.

4How we use your information

We use the information above to:

  • Create and secure your account and authenticate you when you log in.
  • Show you other users' items to browse, filtered by your chosen distance radius.
  • Record your swipes, create matches when interest is mutual, and enable messaging between matched users.
  • Display your listings and profile to other users so swaps can happen.
  • Send you in-app notifications about relevant activity.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the Service, and keep it safe from fraud, abuse, and misuse.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not use your personal information to make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you through solely automated means.

8Location information

Swapt uses location in two ways. First, you can type a location (like a city) into your profile — this is text you choose to share. Second, the optional map view can request your device's approximate coordinates from your browser or operating system to draw the circle that represents your search radius.

We only access device location after you grant permission through your device's standard prompt. If you decline, the map falls back to a default area and Swapt keeps working. You can turn location access off at any time in your device or browser settings.

9Information visible to other users

Swapt is a community marketplace, so some information is meant to be seen by others:

  • Your display name and profile location.
  • Any social handles you choose to add.
  • Your item listings, including photos, descriptions, and tags.
  • Your messages, which are shared with users you've matched with.

Please treat photos, descriptions, and messages as information you're comfortable sharing with the people you might trade with. Avoid posting sensitive personal details in listings or chats.

15Your GDPR rights (EU/UK)

We will respond to requests within one month, as required by the GDPR. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority — in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk); in the EU, your national data-protection authority.
